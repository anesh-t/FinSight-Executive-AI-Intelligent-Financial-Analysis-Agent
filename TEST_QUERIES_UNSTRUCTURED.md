## ✅ Validation Criteria

For each query, check:
1. **Document Retrieval:** Relevant documents/chunks are retrieved
2. **Answer Display:** Response is shown in the chat interface
3. **Answer Quality:** Response is coherent and relevant
4. **Citations:** Document sources are cited (10-K filing, section, page)
5. **No Hallucination:** Information matches source documents
6. **Response Time:** Query completes in < 10 seconds

## 📊 Expected Results Format

### Example 1: Strategic Priorities
**Query:** What are Apple's strategic priorities in 2023?

**Expected Output:**
```
Based on Apple's 2023 10-K filing, their strategic priorities include:

1. **Services Growth**: Expanding Apple Music, iCloud, App Store, and other 
   subscription services to drive recurring revenue.

2. **Privacy & Security**: Maintaining user trust through industry-leading 
   privacy features and data protection.

3. **Ecosystem Integration**: Creating seamless experiences across iPhone, 
   iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and other devices.

4. **Sustainability**: Achieving carbon neutrality across entire business 
   by 2030, including supply chain.

5. **Innovation**: Investing in AR/VR technologies, AI integration, and 
   next-generation products.

Key Quote: "We believe our focus on innovation, privacy, and environmental 
responsibility positions us well for long-term growth."

Source: Apple Inc. 10-K Filing (FY 2023), Business Strategy section, pages 3-5
```

### Example 2: Business Model
**Query:** Explain Apple's business model

**Expected Output:**
```
Apple's business model is based on a vertically integrated ecosystem:

**Hardware Products (70% of revenue):**
- iPhone: Premium smartphones with iOS
- Mac: Personal computers and laptops
- iPad: Tablets for productivity and entertainment
- Wearables: Apple Watch, AirPods, accessories

**Services (30% of revenue):**
- App Store: 30% commission on app sales
- Apple Music: Subscription streaming service
- iCloud: Cloud storage subscriptions
- Apple Pay: Transaction fees
- AppleCare: Extended warranty services

**Key Differentiators:**
- Ecosystem lock-in: Products work seamlessly together
- Premium pricing: High margins on hardware
- Recurring revenue: Growing services business
- Brand loyalty: High customer retention

Source: Apple Inc. 10-K Filing (FY 2023), Business Description, pages 1-3
```

### Example 3: Risk Factors
**Query:** What are Apple's main risks in 2023?

**Expected Output:**
```
Apple's 2023 10-K filing identifies several key risk factors:

**1. Supply Chain Risks:**
- Dependence on single-source suppliers for key components
- Geopolitical tensions affecting manufacturing in China
- Potential disruptions from natural disasters or pandemics

**2. Competitive Risks:**
- Intense competition in smartphones, tablets, and services
- Rapid technological change requiring continuous innovation
- Pricing pressure from lower-cost alternatives

**3. Regulatory Risks:**
- Antitrust investigations in multiple jurisdictions
- App Store policies under regulatory scrutiny
- Data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA)

**4. Market Risks:**
- Dependence on iPhone for majority of revenue
- Economic downturns affecting consumer spending
- Foreign exchange rate fluctuations

**5. Intellectual Property Risks:**
- Patent infringement claims
- Protecting proprietary technology

Source: Apple Inc. 10-K Filing (FY 2023), Risk Factors section, pages 8-15
```
